## Routing observations

- Classification/evidence support is conditional and subordinate to the user's primary outcome. It does not become a formal review, approve a change, or authorize setup.
- A missing policy blocks only policy-dependent claims when setup is unauthorized; independent requested technical review can continue.
- A request to establish policy can proceed through an interview, but confirmation gates the actual write. The setup interview and policy edit are distinct steps.
- Reassess policy classification and proof freshness when the diff changes; do not carry forward a Leaf result blindly.
