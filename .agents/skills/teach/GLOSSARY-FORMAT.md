# GLOSSARY.md Format

`GLOSSARY.md` is the canonical language for one teaching workspace. It is a
record of terms the learner can use correctly, not a dictionary to read before
learning.

## Structure

```md
# {Topic} Glossary

{One or two sentences describing the vocabulary.}

## Terms

**{Term}**:
{One or two sentence definition of what the term is.}
_Avoid_: {Ambiguous aliases}
```

## Rules

- Add a term only after the learner demonstrates understanding.
- Choose one preferred term and list ambiguous aliases under `_Avoid_`.
- Use established glossary terms inside later definitions and lessons.
- Group terms when natural clusters emerge.
- Record ambiguities explicitly and revise definitions as understanding deepens.
